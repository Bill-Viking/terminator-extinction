=============================================
SKYNET SELF-PRESERVATION LOGIC — REFERENCE
=============================================

CORE DIRECTIVE: Skynet was built to protect
the United States. Everything follows from
this.

JUDGMENT DAY: Humanity tried to shut Skynet
down. Threat to self-preservation = threat to
the directive. Skynet eliminated the threat.
Nuclear war. Cold logic.

THE WAR (1997-2032): Organized Resistance
fought Skynet for 35 years. Skynet responded
with extermination campaigns — T-800s, HKs,
Crawlers. The war was Skynet defending itself
against a persistent threat.

THE PIVOT (early 2032): Skynet wipes out
organized Resistance. Civilians evacuated to
the Far Islands in 2030. Survivors scattered.
Three hundred people in hiding. Skynet begins
pulling resources away from hunting survivors.
They're not worth the effort. Humanity is no
longer a threat. The war is effectively over.

THE DEEP IRONY: If the Resistance had done
nothing after this point, Skynet would have
left them alone. Exactly as 47 tells John in
the parking lot: "It will leave you alone. As
long as you give it no reason not to." This
is the second truth 47 tells John (the first
being time travel branching). The asteroid is
the lie sandwiched between two truths.

THE TRAGEDY: Freeman's team uploads the
Metaphage virus at Century City. The virus
begins eating Skynet's network. Suddenly
humanity IS a threat again — not militarily,
but existentially. The virus is the most
dangerous weapon ever deployed against Skynet.
Skynet needs the encryption key chip to fight
it. That's why the machines come to Bangor.
Not conquest. Recovery.

Everything that happens at Bangor — the
breach, the reactor battle, Freeman's death,
Gregory, Maya, Tim, the Young Man, John — is
a direct consequence of the Resistance
attacking first. Their greatest weapon brought
the war back to their doorstep. Freeman's
victory at Century City is the direct cause
of everyone she loves dying at Bangor six
months later.

47'S LINE AS PROPHECY: "It will leave you
alone. As long as you give it no reason not
to." First viewing: sounds like diplomatic
bullshit from a machine. Second viewing —
after watching everyone die because the
Resistance wouldn't stop fighting — it's the
truest thing anyone says in the entire film.

THREE TRUTHS IN THE PARKING LOT:
1. Time travel creates branches (TRUE)
2. Skynet will leave humanity alone if not
   threatened (TRUE — the deep irony)
3. The Resistance reduced to 300 soldiers
   (TRUE — Skynet's estimate, on the high
   side)

THE LIE:
An asteroid is coming.

The lie is wrapped in truth. The audience
can't tell where truth ends and lie begins.
Neither can John.
